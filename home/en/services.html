Services

Five ways we help you build technology that works.

Whether you need a one-off assessment or an ongoing sparring partner, we scope engagements around what you actually need — nothing more, nothing less.

Enterprise Architecture

We help you introduce Enterprise Architecture practices from the ground up, or mature what you already have.

  • EA maturity assessments
  • Introducing frameworks and governance that fit your size
  • Aligning business, application, and technology layers

AI Strategy

Before investing in AI, know where you stand and where the real opportunities are.

  • AI readiness checks
  • AI assessments across data, tooling, and team capability
  • Business use case development, including Design Thinking workshops

CTO Consulting

A sparring partner for CTOs and tech leaders navigating strategy, hiring, architecture, or vendor decisions.

  • Strategy development and validation
  • Second opinion on architecture and technology decisions
  • Support across the many hats of the CTO role

Fractional CTO

We take on CTO responsibilities part-time, structured through our Audit → Strategy → Scale framework.

  • Hands-on technology leadership without a full-time hire
  • A clear, time-boxed engagement structure
  • Built-in mentoring so your team can operate independently

AI-Leadership & Innovation Mindset

Technology only moves as fast as the culture around it. We introduce best practices for team leaders and innovation teams so AI adoption and innovation actually stick.

  • Leadership coaching on AI-driven decision making
  • Building an innovation-ready mindset across teams
  • Practical workshops grounded in real business problems, not theory
How we work together

Engagements that fit the problem, not the other way around.

Project-based

A fixed scope and fee for a defined outcome — for example, an AI readiness assessment or an EA maturity review.

Retainer / ongoing

An ongoing, part-time commitment — the model behind our Fractional CTO engagements.

Hourly / day-rate advisory

Ad-hoc sparring-partner support billed by time, for when you need a second opinion, not a project.

We don't publish rates — every engagement starts with a conversation about what fits your situation.

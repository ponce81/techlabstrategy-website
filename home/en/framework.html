Fractional CTO Framework

Audit. Strategy. Scale.

A structured, twelve-week path to understanding where your technology stands today and building the leadership and roadmap to take it further — without the guesswork.

Note: this framework is under active refinement as we apply it in real engagements — the core structure below reflects our current approach.

Phase 14 weeks

Audit

We start by understanding the reality on the ground — not the org chart version of it.

  • Meet the team
  • Assess the company culture
  • Assess the current tech stack
  • Review documented processes
Deliverable: a findings report highlighting results and possible quick wins.
Phase 24 weeks

Strategy

With a clear picture in hand, we turn findings into a concrete plan.

  • Build a roadmap with specific, actionable steps
  • Determine team needs — hiring gaps or upskilling requirements
Deliverable: an actionable roadmap.
Phase 34 weeks

Scale

The final phase is about making progress durable — so results don't depend on us staying involved.

  • Establish KPIs and metrics
  • Define steps to improve company culture
  • Mentor and train internal tech leads so key players can operate independently
Deliverable: an executive reporting pack for C-level and the board.

Why a structured framework?

Because "trust us, we know what we're doing" isn't a plan. Three clear phases, each four weeks, each with its own deliverable, means you always know where things stand — and what happens next.

Why fractional?

You get hands-on technology leadership without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire — and with mentoring built in from day one, so the capability stays in-house long after the engagement ends.

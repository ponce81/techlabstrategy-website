About

Hi, I'm Carlos Ponce.

Founder of TechStrategyLab — technologist first, strategist second, and a believer that good consulting should feel like a conversation, not a performance.

18 years, one thread: solving real problems

I've spent 18 years in the SAP consulting world, working my way up from hands-on developer to enterprise architect. That path matters — it means I've written the code, integrated the systems, and later designed the architectures that others have to live with. I know what "good in theory" looks like when it meets production.

Most of that experience is rooted in Utilities and Energy companies across Germany and Switzerland — but TechStrategyLab is built to be industry-agnostic. The problems of technology strategy, architecture, and AI readiness look remarkably similar no matter the sector.
